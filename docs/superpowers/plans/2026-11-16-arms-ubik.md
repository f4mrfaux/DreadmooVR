# Arms: UBIK solver port Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans.

> **Status (2026-11-17): Implemented.** Lua UBIK helpers, per-frame arm solve, calibration,
> and capped shoulder follow are in `src/scripts/lib/ubik.lua` and `src/scripts/m2_arms.lua`.
> The AnimBP consumes elbow targets and shoulder translations. Follow-up headset checks and
> current evidence are in `docs/research/2026-11-17-arms-shoulder-review.md`. Keep this plan as
> the historical design record; use that review for current tuning and validation.

**Goal:** Replace our hand-made elbow and shoulder heuristics with the proven UBIK / VRArmIK
algorithm (Parger's thesis, TU Graz; UBIKSolver, MIT, © 2023 Jonas Mølgaard), and add an
automatic arm-span calibration, following the VRIK/FRIK convention of calibrating before IK.

**Why:** Bob asked to follow known-good conventions instead of reinventing them (2026-11-16).

## Design (ruling)

UBIK's node is native C++ and can't go in a pak. Its maths is ported to **Lua**
(`lib/ubik.lua`, pure and desk-tested).
- The bridge already writes `BP_VRArms` variables every frame.
- The output feeds the existing AnimBP **instead of replacing bone rotations**:
  - the elbow world position becomes the Two-Bone IK joint target
  - the clavicle rotation becomes the shoulder offset (world translation of the clavicle,
    from rotating the upper arm root about the clavicle)

  Both are independent of bone axes, so the mannequin/game bone-axis differences don't matter.
- **Cost if wrong:** the Lua cost per frame is a few dozen vector operations, which is
  negligible. If it isn't good enough, move the same module into `DreadmoorVR.dll`.

**Ported** (`AnimNode_UBIKSolver.cpp` line refs; defaults from `UBIK.h`):
- elbow swivel base angle: `RotateElbowByHandPosition` L608–618, with
  ElbowBaseOffsetAngle=90, ElbowYWeight=130, ElbowYDistanceStart=0.2
- hand-twist influence: `RotateElbowByHandRotation` L620–643, ElbowRotFromHandRotAlpha=0.6,
  smoothed by `FInterpTo` with speed 15, `SafeguardAngle` 120 (L527–529)
- elbow on the circle about shoulder→hand via the cosine rule: `SetElbowBasePosition`
  L580–606, `RotateElbow` L645
- clavicle yaw/roll: `RotateUpperArm` L450–479, multiplier 60, limit 45
- torso yaw: head/hand blend 0.75, `GetShoulderRotationFromHands` L374–422

**Calibration:** automatic. Track the widest hand-to-hand span seen; the player's arm =
(span − shoulder width) / 2. That replaces the hand-set `arm_length`. No buttons.

## Tasks

1. `lib/ubik.lua` and `tests/test_ubik.lua` (pure).
2. `m2_arms.lua`: each frame, compute and write 12 floats to `BP_VRArms`
   (`LElbowX/Y/Z`, `RElbowX/Y/Z`, `LShoulderX/Y/Z`, `RShoulderX/Y/Z`).
3. Editor (MCP): `BP_VRArms` gets the 12 floats. `ABP_VRArms` joint targets and shoulder
   shifts read them through MakeVector, replacing the internal elbow and shoulder maths.
   Compile, cook, deploy.
4. Headset check through the harness: reach error, out-of-reach %, elbow behaviour, and
   Bob's verdict.
